Sustainable dentistry paper selected as cover art for RSC Sustainability

The review examines the plastic burden of oral healthcare and the material and practice changes needed to move toward greater circularity.

The paper “The Global Burden of Plastics in Oral Health: Prospects for Circularity, Sustainable Materials Development, and Practice,” led by Jason Y. C. Lim and Vinicius Rosa, was selected as cover art for RSC Sustainability.

The article maps how plastics enter dental care, why short service lives and disposal practices contribute to waste, and where material design and clinical practice could reduce that burden. It discusses alternative polymers, more deliberate use of disposable plastics and circularity strategies as complementary routes toward lower-impact oral healthcare.
