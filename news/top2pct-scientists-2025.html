A/P Rosa ranked among the world’s top 2% most-cited scientists (2025)

Recognised for a fifth consecutive year, with the Dentistry rank improving to #738 among 95,125 authors — approximately the top 0.78% of the subfield.

A/P Vinicius Rosa has been included for the fifth consecutive year in the Stanford–Elsevier database of top-cited scientists, based on standardised bibliometric indicators derived from Scopus.

Within SubField 1 (Dentistry), Rosa ranked #738 among 95,125 authors in the 2025 edition, placing him within approximately the top 0.78% of the subfield. This represents a further improvement from #794 among 89,635 authors in 2024 — 56 positions higher while the Dentistry comparison pool expanded by another 5,490 authors.

The longer trajectory is more informative than a single annual result. From 2022 to 2025, the Dentistry position improved from #1,075 to #738 — a gain of 337 places — while the comparison pool grew from 79,530 to 95,125 authors. Over the same period, the relative position strengthened from approximately the top 1.35% to the top 0.78% of Dentistry.

The Stanford–Elsevier database uses standardised citation indicators rather than simply counting publications or raw citations. Its composite c-score focuses on citation impact and incorporates information on co-authorship and authorship position. As with all bibliometric indicators, the ranking should be interpreted as one measure of research impact rather than a comprehensive measure of research quality.
