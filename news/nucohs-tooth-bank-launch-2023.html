NUS Dentistry presents Singapore's first Tooth Tissue Bank

The NUCOHS Tooth Tissue Bank was established to turn donated extracted teeth into a shared, traceable resource for dental research.

NUS Dentistry introduced the NUCOHS Tooth Tissue Bank, established with NUCOHS to provide researchers with a ready supply of donated extracted teeth for approved studies.

Managed by the Clinical Research Unit, the Bank coordinates donation, cleaning, disinfection, classification, storage and inventory. The model is intended to reduce the time researchers spend collecting samples while enabling work on dental materials, caries, hard-tissue degradation and dental pulp regeneration within a governed tissue-repository framework.

About the Tooth Tissue Bank

The NUCOHS Tooth Tissue Bank at the National University Centre for Oral Health Singapore systematically collects dental pulp and periodontal ligament tissue from extracted teeth donated by consenting patients. The tissue is cryopreserved and made available to researchers studying dental stem cells, tissue engineering, and regenerative endodontics.
