Clarice Sabino Receives the IADR Hatton Award SEA in the Senior Category

The award recognises work using digital holography to study the biological performance of dental cements.

Clarice Sabino received the IADR Hatton Award SEA in the Senior Category at the 2026 IADR Southeast Asia meeting in Phnom Penh, Cambodia.

The recognised project, “Digital BioScore: Integrating Materials and Cells for AI-driven Biomaterials Design,” transforms dental pulp stem-cell responses into a digital holographic representation. The approach provides a way to study the biological performance of dental cements through the responses they elicit from cells.

The work was conducted with collaborators Professor Nick Silikas and Anastasiia Grymak, under the supervision of A/P Vinicius Rosa.
