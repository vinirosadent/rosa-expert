A/P Rosa ranked among the world’s top 2% most-cited scientists (2022)

Recognised for a second consecutive year in the Stanford–Elsevier database, with a Dentistry subfield rank of #1,075 among 79,530 authors.

A/P Vinicius Rosa was included for a second consecutive year in the Stanford–Elsevier database of top-cited scientists, which uses standardised bibliometric indicators derived from Scopus to compare citation impact across scientific fields and subfields.

Dentistry baseline
2022 #1,075 / 79,530 Top 1.35%

This is the first Dentistry subfield value used in the site’s year-to-year trajectory.

Within SubField 1 (Dentistry), Rosa ranked #1,075 among 79,530 authors, corresponding to approximately the top 1.35% of the field. This 2022 result provides the baseline for the upward Dentistry trajectory seen in the following editions.

The database is not a simple count of publications or raw citations. Its composite c-score focuses on citation impact and incorporates information on co-authorship and authorship position alongside other standardised indicators. As with any bibliometric measure, the result is best interpreted as one indicator of research impact rather than a comprehensive measure of research quality.
