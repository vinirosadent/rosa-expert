A/P Rosa begins term as President of the IADR Dental Materials Group

The role places him at the head of IADR's international community focused on dental materials research.

A/P Vinicius Rosa began his term as President of the IADR Dental Materials Group in June 2023, following the IADR/LAR General Session in Bogotá, Colombia.

As President, Rosa aimed to strengthen connections between established and emerging researchers and expand opportunities for collaboration across dental materials science. The appointment followed nearly two decades of involvement with IADR, beginning as an undergraduate attendee in 2005 and later including service on the IADR Board of Directors.

About the IADR DMG

The IADR Dental Materials Group (DMG) is one of the largest and most active divisions of the IADR. It organises symposia and award sessions at the IADR General Sessions and promotes international collaboration and career development in dental materials research.
