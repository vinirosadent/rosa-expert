A/P Rosa joins CNA’s Talking Point investigation into toothpaste efficacy

Channel News Asia’s Talking Point investigated whether premium toothpastes deliver better oral care — or simply better marketing.

In August 2025, A/P Vinicius Rosa contributed to Talking Point, Channel News Asia’s (CNA) current-affairs programme, for an episode examining a deceptively simple consumer question: when one toothpaste can cost several times more than another, are consumers actually paying for better oral care?

Hosted by Steven Chia, the episode — Is My Toothpaste Worth The Price?, released on YouTube as How Effective Is Your Toothpaste? — examined products marketed for whitening, gum health, enamel protection, sensitivity and other specialised benefits. The programme investigated what these claims mean, how strongly they are supported by science, and whether price is a useful indicator of effectiveness.

A/P Rosa joined the programme as a dental materials expert to explain the science behind toothpaste formulations, including what goes into a typical toothpaste and the central role of fluoride in protecting teeth. His contribution helped connect the chemistry of these everyday products with their biological and clinical purpose.

The discussion also addressed a broader point raised throughout the investigation: there is no single “best” toothpaste based simply on price or on the number of claims printed on the packaging. Different formulations may contain ingredients intended for specific oral-health needs, while a higher price does not by itself indicate greater effectiveness.

The feature illustrates how materials science can help consumers look beyond product claims and understand what an oral-care formulation is actually designed to do. For Rosa, whose research spans dental biomaterials, biocompatibility and the development of new oral-health technologies, the programme provided an opportunity to translate scientific evidence into decisions people make every day.

The 23-minute episode aired on CNA’s Talking Point on 3 August 2025 and is available through CNA and CNA Insider.
