A/P Rosa joins the IADR Board of Directors

He served a two-year term as the Young Investigator Representative from 2021 to 2023.

A/P Vinicius Rosa joined the Board of Directors of the International Association for Dental Research (IADR) as Young Investigator Representative, serving from 2021 to 2023.

The Board brings together elected officers, regional representatives, journal leadership and other members responsible for IADR governance and strategic direction. Rosa’s Board service followed his selection for the 2021 IADR Distinguished Scientist Young Investigator Award, whose recipient also serves a two-year Board term.
