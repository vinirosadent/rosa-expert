A/P Rosa joins the Editorial Board of the European Journal of Oral Sciences

The appointment adds a new editorial role in a journal spanning oral biology, clinical dentistry and related sciences.

A/P Vinicius Rosa was appointed to the Editorial Board of the European Journal of Oral Sciences in October 2024.

The journal is the official publication of the Scandinavian Division of the International Association for Dental, Oral, and Craniofacial Research. The appointment expands Rosa's editorial service across dental materials, oral sciences and translational research.

EJOS covers the full breadth of oral sciences — from cellular biology to clinical outcomes.

A/P Rosa's appointment reflects his multidisciplinary contributions to dental materials, nanotechnology, tissue engineering and biocompatibility — fields that align with the journal's commitment to integrative, cross-disciplinary oral science research.
