NUHS+ goes behind the scenes at the NUCOHS Tooth Tissue Bank

The feature follows how donated extracted teeth are collected, cleaned, catalogued and prepared for research, and looks at where the Bank could expand next.

NUHS+ featured the daily operations of the NUCOHS Tooth Tissue Bank, following the path of donated extracted teeth from clinical collection through cleaning, classification, storage and preparation for specific research protocols.

A/P Vinicius Rosa described the Bank as shared research infrastructure designed to reduce delays in specimen collection and support work ranging from dental materials testing to pulp regeneration. The feature also outlined a broader vision for future biobanking that could include soft tissues, stem-cell-derived samples, saliva and tumour biopsies, extending the platform beyond teeth alone.

About the Tooth Tissue Bank

The NUCOHS Tooth Tissue Bank at the National University Centre for Oral Health Singapore systematically collects dental pulp and periodontal ligament tissue from extracted teeth donated by consenting patients. The tissue is cryopreserved and made available to researchers studying dental stem cells, tissue engineering, and regenerative endodontics.
