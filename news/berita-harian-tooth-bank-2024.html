Berita Harian features the NUCOHS Tooth Tissue Bank

Singapore’s Malay-language daily highlighted how donated extracted teeth are supporting oral-health research at NUS Dentistry and NUCOHS.

Berita Harian featured the NUCOHS Tooth Tissue Bank in September 2024, bringing the research infrastructure behind donated extracted teeth to a wider Singapore audience.

Established in 2021, the Tooth Tissue Bank provides researchers with prepared tooth specimens for studies that would otherwise require lengthy sample collection. The coverage highlighted how this shared resource supports research at NUS Dentistry and NUCOHS, from dental materials testing to regenerative approaches using cells and tissues derived from teeth.

A tooth can outlive the patient — and that permanence is the foundation of the bank.

The NUCOHS Tooth Tissue Bank represents an important scientific infrastructure investment for Singapore. By systematically collecting tissue from procedures at the National University Centre for Oral Health Singapore, it creates a long-term resource for human dental stem cell research — a cornerstone of efforts to develop biologically based regenerative therapies for the dental pulp and periodontium. The bank also positions Singapore as a regional hub for dental regenerative science.

About the Tooth Tissue Bank

The NUCOHS Tooth Tissue Bank collects dental pulp and periodontal ligament tissue from extracted teeth donated by patients at the National University Centre for Oral Health Singapore. The tissue is cryopreserved and made available to researchers studying dental stem cells, tissue engineering, and regenerative endodontics.
