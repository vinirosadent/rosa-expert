A/P Rosa joins the Editorial Board of Discover Nano

The appointment extends Rosa's editorial work into nanoscience and nanotechnology.

A/P Vinicius Rosa was appointed to the Editorial Board of Discover Nano in September 2024.

Discover Nano is an open-access journal covering research across nanoscience and nanotechnology and was formerly published as Nanoscale Research Letters. The appointment complements Rosa's research and editorial activities at the interface of dental materials, nanomaterials and biomedical applications.

Discover Nano covers the full spectrum of nanoscience and its real-world applications.

A/P Rosa's appointment is grounded in his extensive work on graphene-based nanomaterials for dental and biomedical applications — including graphene nanocoatings for implants, graphene substrates for cell differentiation and the development of nanocomposites for dental biomaterials.
