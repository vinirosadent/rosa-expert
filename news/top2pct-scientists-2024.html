A/P Rosa ranked among the world’s top 2% most-cited scientists (2024)

A fourth consecutive inclusion, with a further rise in Dentistry from #832 to #794 while the comparison pool continued to expand.

A/P Vinicius Rosa was included for a fourth consecutive year in the Stanford–Elsevier database of top-cited scientists, based on standardised bibliometric indicators derived from Scopus.

Continued improvement
2023 #832 / 83,997 Top 0.99%
+38
2024 #794 / 89,635 Top 0.89%

38 positions higher while the Dentistry comparison pool grew by 5,638 authors.

Within SubField 1 (Dentistry), Rosa moved from #832 among 83,997 authors in 2023 to #794 among 89,635 authors in 2024. The rank improved by a further 38 positions even as the Dentistry comparison pool grew by 5,638 authors, strengthening his relative position from approximately the top 0.99% to the top 0.89% of the subfield.

The result extends the trajectory established in 2022 and 2023. The Stanford–Elsevier database combines citation impact, co-authorship and authorship-position information through standardised indicators and a composite c-score, rather than treating publication volume or raw citation counts as a standalone measure.
