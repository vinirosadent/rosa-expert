A/P Rosa ranked among the world’s top 2% most-cited scientists (2023)

A third consecutive inclusion, with the Dentistry rank rising from #1,075 to #832 and moving into the top 1% of the subfield.

A/P Vinicius Rosa was included for a third consecutive year in the Stanford–Elsevier database of top-cited scientists, based on standardised bibliometric indicators derived from Scopus.

2023 #832 / 83,997 Top 0.99%

243 positions higher while the Dentistry comparison pool grew by 4,467 authors.

Within SubField 1 (Dentistry), Rosa advanced from #1,075 among 79,530 authors in 2022 to #832 among 83,997 authors in 2023. This represents an improvement of 243 ranking positions while the comparison pool expanded by 4,467 authors, moving his relative position from approximately the top 1.35% to the top 0.99% of Dentistry — within the top 1% of the subfield.

The database combines several standardised citation indicators rather than relying only on publication counts or raw citations. Its composite c-score focuses on citation impact and incorporates information on co-authorship and authorship position. The result should therefore be viewed as one bibliometric indicator of research impact.
