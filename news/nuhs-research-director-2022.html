A/P Rosa appointed Research Director at NUHS

The appointment links Faculty-level research strategy with broader planning and development across the National University Health System.

A/P Vinicius Rosa was appointed Research Director at the National University Health System (NUHS), effective 1 July 2022.

In the role, he worked with NUHS research leadership, senior administration and other stakeholders on strategic planning, evaluation and development of new research initiatives, while continuing to support research within the NUS Faculty of Dentistry. The appointment ran through 30 June 2025.
