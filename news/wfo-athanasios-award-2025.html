NUS master's thesis receives WFO Athanasios E. Athanasiou Award

Master's research by Watt Sook May, co-supervised at NUS, was selected for the World Federation of Orthodontists' 2024–25 thesis award.

The master’s thesis “Effect of bio-aging on properties of conventional acrylic and 3D printed resins,” authored by NUS student Watt Sook May, was selected as a recipient of the 2024–25 Athanasios E. Athanasiou Master’s Thesis Award from the World Federation of Orthodontists (WFO).

The work examines how artificial ageing changes the properties of conventional acrylic and 3D-printed resins used in orthodontic appliances, linking material performance to both conventional and digital workflows. The thesis was co-supervised by Dr Eugene Loh, A/P Kelvin Foong and A/P Vinicius Rosa. The WFO presents the award annually to recognise outstanding master’s-level research in orthodontics.
