A/P Rosa is appointed to the Editorial Board of the Journal of Dental Research

The Journal of Dental Research is the flagship peer-reviewed publication of the International Association for Dental, Oral and Craniofacial Research.

Associate Professor Vinicius Rosa was appointed to the Editorial Board of the Journal of Dental Research (JDR) and continues to serve in the role today. Published by SAGE on behalf of the International Association for Dental, Oral and Craniofacial Research (IADR), the JDR is the Association’s flagship journal and a leading peer-reviewed venue in dentistry and oral sciences.

The JDR covers oral biology, dental materials, tissue engineering, implantology, caries research and clinical sciences, and prioritises original research with clear scientific merit and translational potential.

The JDR bridges laboratory discovery and clinical translation across dental sciences.

Serving on an editorial board is a form of stewardship for the field. Board members evaluate submissions, help set the standards of rigour and reporting applied to published work, and advise on which findings are ready to inform further research and clinical practice. Peer review is largely invisible to readers, yet the reliability of what a journal publishes rests on it, and it depends on reviewers who can judge evidence across disciplines.

A/P Rosa contributes his expertise in dental materials, graphene nanotechnology and dental pulp tissue engineering to this process, supporting the journal’s commitment to rigorous, interdisciplinary evaluation at the intersection of materials science, regenerative dentistry and biocompatibility research.
