Precision detection of biomarker concentration in saliva and sweat

A graphene biomarker analyzer aims to replace traditional blood tests with more convenient sweat and saliva tests.

The project on quantifying biomarkers from blood and saliva has successfully delivered the first proof-of-concept prototypes for their graphene biomarker analyzer. This innovative technology aims to replace traditional blood tests with more convenient sweat and saliva tests for precisely detecting biomarker concentrations.

Supported by the National Research Foundation Singapore led by Prof. AH Castro Neto, NanoMolar’s graphene-based sensor provides unparalleled accuracy in identifying various biomarkers, including electrolytes, metabolites, vitamins, hormones, proteins, peptides, and xenobiotics in sweat and saliva.

D+I has developed an optimized three-component system with a simple workflow. This system delivers real-time readings through a Smart Device Application, with calibrated concentration results available in under five minutes.
