Clarice Sabino Receives the Academy of Dental Materials Paffenbarger Award

The award recognises an AI-driven atlas for screening the biological performance of calcium silicate cements.

Clarice Sabino received the George C. Paffenbarger Award of the Academy of Dental Materials at the Academy’s meeting in Tokyo, Japan, on 9 October 2026. The recognition comes only two weeks after she received the IADR-SEA Hatton Award in the Senior Category.

The recognised project, “Novel AI-Driven Hyperdimensional Regenerative Atlas for Screening CSCs Biological Performance” (HYDRA), is an AI-driven approach to screening the biological performance of calcium silicate cements (CSCs).

The work was conducted with collaborators Professor Nick Silikas and Anastasiia Grymak, under the supervision of A/P Vinicius Rosa.

The award carries a particular meaning for A/P Rosa, who received the same award in 2007 — 19 years before Clarice.

About the award

The George C. Paffenbarger Award is part of the Academy of Dental Materials’ programmes that encourage students to conduct research in dental materials. It has been awarded continuously by the Academy since 1986.
