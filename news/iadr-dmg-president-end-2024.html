A/P Rosa completes term as President of the IADR Dental Materials Group

The 2023–24 presidency included changes to the Group's structure and the creation of an award focused on international research collaboration.

A/P Vinicius Rosa completed his term as President of the IADR Dental Materials Group (DMG) during the 2024 IADR General Session in New Orleans, where he received a commemorative plaque from DMG Secretary Dr Alvaro Della Bona.

During the presidency, Rosa initiated a modernization of the Group's subgroups and established the David C. Watts Award in International Collaboration to recognise leadership in cross-border dental materials research. He subsequently moved into the Past-President role and continued to contribute to the Group's awards activities.

Leadership in science is about creating space for others to do their best work.

The IADR Dental Materials Group has played a central role in shaping the scientific agenda of dental materials research since its founding. Past presidents have included some of the most influential figures in the history of dental materials science. A/P Rosa’s service at the helm of this group adds an important chapter to his record of leadership in international dental research organisations and reflects the global reach and influence of his research programme.

About the IADR DMG

The IADR Dental Materials Group (DMG) is one of the largest and most active divisions of the IADR. It organises symposia and award sessions at the IADR General Sessions and promotes international collaboration and career development in dental materials research.
