Clarice Sabino receives the IADR Kulzer Travel Award 2023

The award, sponsored by Kulzer and granted by the International Association for Dental, Oral and Craniofacial Research, recognises emerging researchers in dental biomaterials and supports their participation in the IADR Annual Meeting.

Clarice Sabino, a PhD student in the research group of Associate Professor Vinicius Rosa at the NUS Faculty of Dentistry, received the IADR Kulzer Travel Award 2023. The award is sponsored by Kulzer and granted by the International Association for Dental, Oral and Craniofacial Research (IADR) to support promising researchers in attending and presenting their work at the IADR Annual Meeting.

The IADR travel award programme aims to foster scientific exchange and accelerate the careers of emerging talent in dental research. Recipients are selected based on the scientific quality and originality of their submitted abstracts, making the recognition a mark of research excellence at the international level.

Recognising emerging talent and supporting scientific exchange at the highest international level.

The distinction reflects the high standard of research produced by A/P Rosa’s laboratory at NUS Dentistry, where students consistently earn recognition from leading international scientific organisations. The IADR, with members in over 90 countries, is the world’s largest international dental research organisation and its awards carry significant prestige within the global dental research community.
