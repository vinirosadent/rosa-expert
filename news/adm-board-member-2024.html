A/P Rosa appointed to the Board of Directors of the Academy of Dental Materials

The three-year appointment extends Rosa's service to the international dental materials community.

A/P Vinicius Rosa was appointed as a Board Member of the Academy of Dental Materials (ADM) during its annual meeting in Torino, Italy, in October 2024.

The Academy brings together researchers and clinicians working across dental materials science and is closely linked to the journal Dental Materials. Rosa's Board term runs from 2024 to 2027 and adds to his longstanding involvement with the Academy, which began with its Student Award in 2005 and later included the George C. Paffenbarger Award and election as a Fellow.

Board members guide the scientific and institutional direction of the Academy.

As a Board member, A/P Rosa helps set the Academy's global research priorities, coordinate the scientific committees of its international meetings, and support early-career scientists — placing NUS directly within the decisions that shape the future of biomaterials in dentistry.
