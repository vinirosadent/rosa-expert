A/P Rosa is elected Vice-President of the IADR Dental Materials Group

His term starts in July 2021, followed by the roles of President-Elect and President in the subsequent years.

Associate Professor Vinicius Rosa has been elected Vice-President of the International Association for Dental Research Dental Materials Group (DMG). His term will start in July 2021, and he will then take on the roles of President-Elect and President in the subsequent years.

“IADR/DMG has been one of the most traditional research groups since it was established in 1938, and its members have been responsible for most of the material developments and technologies used in clinical practice. It is an honor to work for the development of this group, which is praised for its several world-class researchers,” said A/P Vinicius Rosa.

A/P Rosa has been a member of the IADR since 2005, when he attended the meeting in Baltimore, USA, as an undergraduate student. That year he received the Dental Materials Student Award, which the Academy of Dental Materials presents to the most outstanding student researchers in the field. In 2007 he received the Academy’s George C. Paffenbarger Student Research Award, which recognises the best paper in dental materials each year. He is a member of the editorial boards of Dental Materials, the Journal of Dental Research, the Journal of Prosthodontics and others. Since January 2021, A/P Rosa has led the research portfolio of the NUS Faculty of Dentistry as Vice-Dean (Research).
